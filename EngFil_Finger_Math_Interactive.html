Step 1
📖 How to Use Finger Math

1. Pick Your Numbers

Each finger stands for a number from 6 to 10 (Pinky = 6, Ring = 7, Middle = 8, Index = 9, Thumb = 10). Use the ▲ / ▼ buttons below each hand to choose the two numbers you want to multiply (for example: Left = 8, Right = 7).

2. Find the Tens and Ones

Tens: Count every raised finger on both hands together, then multiply that count by 10.
Ones: On each hand, count the fingers that aren't raised, then multiply the left hand's count by the right hand's count.

3. Add Them Together

Add the Tens value and the Ones value to get your final answer!

💡 Quick Summary

Pick two numbers with your fingers, then do two quick counts: raised fingers × 10 (tens), and un-raised fingers on each hand multiplied together (ones). Add the two results, and that's your answer!
