✖️➗ Multiplying & Dividing Integers

Master the sign rules with small numbers, then practice.

📘 Quick Reminder

Integers are whole numbers that can be positive, negative, or zero. In this lesson we focus only on sign rules using small numbers so you can master the process without big calculations.

✖️ Lesson 1: Multiplying Integers

When multiplying integers, look at the signs of the two numbers.

RULE — SAME SIGNS

If the signs are the same (both positive or both negative):
The product is POSITIVE.

Example 1: Positive × Positive

(+3) × (+2)

Both signs are positive (same).

3 × 2 = 6

Answer: +6

Example 2: Negative × Negative

(-4) × (-2)

Both signs are negative (same).

4 × 2 = 8

Answer: +8

RULE — OPPOSITE SIGNS

If the signs are different (one positive, one negative):
The product is NEGATIVE.

Example 3: Positive × Negative

(+5) × (-3)

Signs are opposite.

5 × 3 = 15

Answer: -15

Example 4: Negative × Positive

(-6) × (+2)

Signs are opposite.

6 × 2 = 12

Answer: -12

🧠 Easy reminder:

Same signs → Positive product
Opposite signs → Negative product

➗ Lesson 2: Dividing Integers

Division of integers follows the same sign rules as multiplication.

RULE — SAME SIGNS

Same signs → The quotient is POSITIVE.

Example 1: Positive ÷ Positive

(+8) ÷ (+2)

Both signs are positive.

8 ÷ 2 = 4

Answer: +4

Example 2: Negative ÷ Negative

(-9) ÷ (-3)

Both signs are negative.

9 ÷ 3 = 3

Answer: +3

RULE — OPPOSITE SIGNS

Opposite signs → The quotient is NEGATIVE.

Example 3: Positive ÷ Negative

(+10) ÷ (-2)

Signs are opposite.

10 ÷ 2 = 5

Answer: -5

Example 4: Negative ÷ Positive

(-12) ÷ (+3)

Signs are opposite.

12 ÷ 3 = 4

Answer: -4

⚠️ Important: You cannot divide by zero.
Any number ÷ 0 is undefined.
🧠 Remember:

Multiplication and Division use the exact same sign rules.
